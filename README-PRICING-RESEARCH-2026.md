## DigitalPulse price model

1. Existing LPCMS Premium / Premium Pro small monthly subscriptions are **unchanged**. They are software/platform subscriptions; bespoke setup is not included.
2. One-off landing pages, WordPress Business and Growth, standard online shops and advanced B2B stores are **separate quoted offers**.
3. Custom WordPress plugins are scoped around a single clearly defined starter feature, with larger integrations on quotation.
4. Bespoke web apps and AI platforms start at a higher professional budget and require discovery and milestones.
5. UK GBP, European EUR and Latvian EUR amounts are **deliberate regional commercial schedules, not spot-FX exchange conversions**. Site language selects presentation; pricing for a particular customer must be confirmed in the written proposal.
6. Each price is a suggested **from-price**, not a binding quote or a statistically measured national median. VAT, hosting, domains, licences and third-party fees depend on the actual sale.

**Internal note:** Before announcing prices publicly, confirm DigitalPulse’s actual delivery hours and margins, hosting/licence obligations, VAT status and support responsibilities. After a sales enquiry, agree currency and region explicitly rather than inferring them from IP or language.
